# 📊 Tata Data Analytics Virtual Internship

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is based on analyzing an online retail dataset to answer key business questions for the CEO and CMO. The goal is to generate meaningful insights that support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions
1. Analyze monthly revenue trends for 2011 and identify seasonality  
2. Identify top 10 countries by revenue (excluding UK)  
3. Find top 10 customers contributing to revenue  
4. Analyze global demand to support expansion strategy  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI  
- Data Cleaning  
- Data Visualization  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Revenue shows strong seasonal growth, especially in Q4  
- Few countries contribute significantly to overall revenue  
- Business depends heavily on top customers  
- High-demand regions offer expansion opportunities
